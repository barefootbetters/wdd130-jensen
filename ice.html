ICE Challenges

In-Class Exercises from WDD 130 — each one a small, focused build. Here's what every challenge asked for and what I took away from it.

Challenge Log

Click any challenge to open it in a new tab.

  • Favorite Devotionals

    Build a page that lists favorite devotional talks in a data table and offers a form to add a new one.

    What I learned: Structuring an accessible table with thead/tbody/th, and wiring up a form with labels, text and number inputs, validation, and a submit button.

    Open challenge →

  • Father's Day Page

    Style a pre-written HTML page for the holiday without changing the markup — all design driven from a single stylesheet.

    What I learned: Theming with CSS alone — layered gradients, ::before pseudo-elements to inject emoji, drop-caps, and hover transitions, all while leaving the HTML untouched.

    Open challenge →

  • Image Gallery

    Optimize a slow gallery of Christ-centered art by shrinking the images and generating small thumbnails.

    What I learned: Why image weight matters — converting 57 MB of PNGs into ~1 MB JPGs, building 200×300 thumbnails, and pointing thumbnails at full-size images with the href/src split.

    Open challenge →

  • Sign Up Form

    Style a data-collection sign-up form and study the JavaScript that previews the submitted data.

    What I learned: Laying out a form with CSS Grid, styling radios/checkboxes with accent-color, and reading a first bit of JavaScript — FormData, event listeners, and preventDefault().

    Open challenge →

  • Prophet Card

    Convert a wireframe into a single styled profile card for President Dallin H. Oaks — photo, key dates, and a quote.

    What I learned: Translating a wireframe straight into HTML/CSS, composing an image with a fact list, and styling a pull-quote inside a self-contained card component.

    Open challenge →

  • Apostle Spotlight

    Lay out the First Presidency and the Quorum of the Twelve as a gallery of captioned portraits.

    What I learned: Using figure and figcaption semantically, building a responsive image grid, and visually featuring one item within the set.

    Open challenge →
