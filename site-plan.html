Site Purpose

A personal portfolio that showcases my web design and development work. Its purpose is to present my projects, skills, and background in a clean, professional way so that a potential employer, client, or collaborator can quickly understand what I can build — and to serve as a living record of my growth as a front-end developer.

Audience

  • Potential employers and clients evaluating a front-end developer
  • Instructors and classmates reviewing my work
  • Anyone learning web development who wants real, hosted examples

Site Structure

A home page plus two child pages. Every page shares the same header, navigation, and footer, and links to both child pages.

  • Home (index.html) — introduction and featured work
  • About Me (about.html) — background, skills, and goals
  • Projects (projects.html) — detailed portfolio of builds

Color Scheme

  • Primary — #2C3E50
  • Secondary — #18BC9C
  • Accent — #F39C12
  • Background — #ECF0F1
  • Surface — #FFFFFF
  • Muted — #7F8C8D

Typography

Montserrat — headings (h1–h6), navigation

Open Sans — body copy, forms, captions

Both are loaded from Google Fonts with system-font fallbacks.

Logo

A circular "JJ" monogram badge in the brand palette — a dark-slate disc, teal ring, and orange accent. Saved as a PNG at images/logo.png and shown in the header on every page.

Wireframes

Layout sketches for the home page and the shared child-page template, with every required element labeled.
