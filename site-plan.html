Site Purpose

A single portal page collecting every project I build during WDD 130, plus an About Me page. It should look like a real portfolio that I could share with an employer or client.

Audience

  • My WDD 130 instructor (for grading)
  • Classmates reviewing my work
  • Future employers or clients reviewing a portfolio

Color Scheme

  • Primary — #2C3E50
  • Secondary — #18BC9C
  • Accent — #F39C12
  • Background — #ECF0F1
  • Surface — #FFFFFF
  • Muted — #7F8C8D

Typography

Montserrat — headings (h1–h6), navigation

Open Sans — body copy, forms, captions

Both are loaded from Google Fonts with system-font fallbacks.

Logo

A circular "JJ" monogram badge in the brand palette — a navy disc, teal ring, and orange accent. Saved as a PNG at images/logo.png and shown in the header on every page.

Full-page layout sketch with every required element labeled.
